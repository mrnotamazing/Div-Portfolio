(02) How I got here

It started small.

I have a habit of turning "what if..." into projects. It started on Fiverr: three years of YouTube thumbnails and logos, plus a channel of my own that passed 80,000 views.

In my first sales internship, at a small clothing boutique, I closed a ₹1,00,000 order in week one and kept it as a repeat account. Later I led a 10-person zero-waste stall at our college EcoFest to ₹35,000+ in a day. (I once got my professors to sign a 'legally binding?' promise to buy from our fundraiser stall, too.)

Now I'm at TAPMI Bengaluru with a 9.0 GPA, on a 100% tuition scholarship that went to the top 5 of 300+ students. This summer: 25+ interviews and 5 prototypes for a fire-safety NGO. Business led me to psychology, then design, then tech, and my team won the Y.CEO Programme at IIT Madras along the way.
